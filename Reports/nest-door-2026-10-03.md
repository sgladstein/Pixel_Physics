# Keeping the lab nest's door open

*Measurement and two switches, 2026-10-03. `engine`/`lab`. Nest lane
(project thread "Nest building"). Follows
[`nest-one-entrance-2026-09-29.md`](nest-one-entrance-2026-09-29.md) and
[`nest-heap-cue-2026-09-28.md`](nest-heap-cue-2026-09-28.md), whose door and
heap cue this changes. The WIP that §1 and §3a were measured on is on
`claude/nest-building-8j7uwn` at `62029b26`..`10f9fc73`; its probes were not
shipped.*

## 0. The answer

**In the evolution lab a colony's door filled in within a few thousand frames
and stayed shut, so the colony lived as a crowd on the ground and dug almost
nothing.** Two rules, both on by default and both genes:

- **Food is not put down in the door** (`PIXEL_PHYSICS_FOOD_DOOR`, slot 24
  `TRAIT_DOOR_CLEAR`). A fed ant at home never sets food down in the
  founding shaft or within 6 cells of it, from 6 rows over its mouth down to
  its foot; it walks it out or hands it through the crowd. **The chamber at
  the shaft's foot is not door**: food put down there is stored in the nest.
- **Re-opening its own door is not starting a new mouth**
  (`PIXEL_PHYSICS_DOOR_REOPEN`, slot 25 `TRAIT_DOOR_CUE`). The heap cue,
  which lets a dig open ground to the sky only beside a heap of spoil, stands
  aside for a cut into the colony's own founding cut.

**Shipped code against main `98eb7fe7`**, 12 paired lab boxes
(`scripts/labbench.py` arms, played bed, 120,000 frames; §3c):

- **more of the colony lives underground: 18.7% -> 23.4% of animals (higher
  on 10 of 12, sign p 0.04)**, the first lever in this lane to move it;
- **eggs laid at home 1.1% -> 3.9% (10 of 12)**, within 8 columns of a nest
  5 -> 18 (11 of 12);
- **alive at the end 53 -> 107.5 (11 of 12, p 0.006)**; boxes that fell below
  a quarter of their peak 4 -> 1, under 10 ants at the end 0 -> 0;
- starved per million ant-frames 13.2 -> 7.5 (lower on 10 of 12);
- births, food eaten and ant-frames lived level (8/4, 7/5, 5/7).

**The first version also barred the chamber, and that cost a colony** (§3b):
on 1 of 12 boxes a colony that had dug little beyond its founding cut had
nowhere at home to put food down, and ended at one ant. Freeing the chamber
fixed that box and lifted the underground share. **The food rule carries the
gain; re-opening alone moves nothing** (§3a); both ship on, neither showing a
measured harm.

**What this does not fix.** Most eggs are still laid far from home; on some
seeds the door still fills, now mostly with water dripping off the lid and
loose soil (§5).

## 1. What shuts the door

All on `labforage scenario=played_bed`, main `d4418bf2`, 4 seeds unless
stated.

- **Shut on 20 of 20 lab runs**; on the food box (`digbox`) the shaft stays
  open. By about 100,000 frames the food box has dug 837-4,503 cells (55-480
  roofed), the lab 74-370 (25-76 roofed).
- **It is not the garden alone.** A lab box with no plants, fed like the food
  box, built a nest on 4 of 8 runs (`feast_heap`/`feast_scatter`, roofed
  64-153, door 19-26 of 26 cells open); on the other 4 the door shut (roofed
  0-12). Played bed: 0 of 4. Spreading the food box's food over its whole
  surface still dug a nest (roofed 144-207 against 22-238 piled).
- **Holding the door open by hand is enough.** An oracle that emptied the
  founding cut every 64 frames: home 107-174 cells against 14-137, dig
  attempts at home higher on 4 of 4. It took out 420-2,327 cells in 36,000
  frames: **46-84% crumbs**, seeds 57-2,358, grass 31-250, water 75-146.
- **Who put the crumbs there**, censused by write site (`DOORWRITES`, WIP
  `c5da7483`): food set down by ants at home, 27-354 crumbs into the shaft by
  36,000 frames. With the shaft kept clear at a margin of 2, food piled on
  the lip slid and fell in: 549 falls and 781 slides of crumbs on seed 1 to
  16,000 frames, and the door still shut on 3 of 4 seeds; hence 6.
- **The colony would not dig it open again.** The heap cue refuses a cut that
  opens ground to the sky with no pellet within 2 cells; a door filled from
  above is exactly that. The dig funnel at home counted **443-5,130 such
  refusals** over 72,000 frames (seeds 1 and 4).

## 2. The two rules

`food_door_of`, `door_clear_of`, `in_doorway` and `food_drop_site`;
`door_reopen_of` and `door_cue_weight` at the heap cue's call in `act`
(`src/sim/creature.rs`). `Reports/how-the-ant-works.md` §5 steps 4 and 6 and
§12 describe them as shipped.

- **Food door.** Neither drop rule (the 8 neighbours, then through bodies),
  nor a nest worker piling food (`store_drop`), counts a cell as room if it lies within
  the clearance of a founding shaft: that many columns either side, from
  that many rows over its mouth down to its foot. Clearance = 6 /
  `ratio_factor(allele)`, rounded: 6 at allele 0, 12 at +1, 3 at -1. The
  chamber under the foot and a side room are outside it.
- **Door reopen.** At a target inside a founding cut (shaft, chamber or side
  room) the cue's chance `f` becomes `1 - w(1 - f)`, `w` = slot 25 clamped to
  0..1. At `w` 0 (shipped) the cue is skipped there, as for a tunnel's
  digger; at `w` 1 it is the cue before.
- **As genes.** Width 0, allele 0, no developmental weight, on every species
  (the walk genes' terms, `organism::DOOR_SLOTS`), so allele 0 is the rules as
  measured and nothing mutates until a measurement turns the width on. The
  lab's genes page shows them as DOOR CLEARING and DOOR HEAP NEED.
- **`=off` is the ant before**, on both switches: with both off the shipped
  build's log equals main `98eb7fe7`'s byte for byte (seed 1, 120,000
  frames). The decision trace's `free8` now counts only neighbours the drop
  would take, door cells excluded, so its agreement test holds.

**Concession** (Scott's engine-review ruling, 2026-10-03: local cues where
feasible). The rules read where the colony's founding cut is
(`World::nest_sites`), not something the ant senses at the cell. A local form
would key on the door scent or on being `AtNest` with open sky overhead; not
built.

## 4. Biology

- Wood ants carry sticks laid over their entrances away (Arscott et al.
  2026, *J. Exp. Biol.*, "Heuristic rules for cooperative transport in wood
  ant nest maintenance",
  <https://consensus.app/papers/details/b744c809d877538284058bae3e9a7da3/>);
  harvester ants keep a cleared disc round the nest (MacMahon et al. 2000;
  Carlson 1991, as cited in the garden lane's
  `/mnt/project-files/garden/garden-balance-2026-10-03.md`), and store seed
  in chambers, not in the entrance (`Reports/nest-granary-2026-09-28.md`).
- Leaf-cutting ants open a new entrance where a heap of soil gives the cue
  (Pielström & Roces 2013), which is what the heap cue models. Re-cutting a
  door the colony already has is not that decision.

## 5. What is still open

- **Water.** Condensation under the lid (`weather::condense_under_a_lid`)
  drips at random columns and pools in the shaft; nothing an ant does takes
  water out. It was 75-146 of the oracle's cells.
- **The packed crowd.** 13-18 ants at a time have no empty cell beside them at
  the door, but they keep moving (at most 3 still for 1,500+ frames, seeds
  1-3, every 100 frames to 72,000). Not a stuck-ant bug.
- **Most of the colony is still above ground** (23%) and only 3.9% of eggs
  are laid at home. The laying lane's walk home is the other half.

## 6. Tried and not shipped

Each is in `Reports/dead-ends.md`. All on main `d4418bf2`, played bed, 4
seeds, 72,000 frames unless stated; all left the door plugged and the nest
unchanged (`62029b26`'s message), except where a figure says otherwise.

- **The food door with the chamber barred too**: §3b. One colony in 12 had
  nowhere at home to put food down.
- **Food set down only on footing** (`FOOD_FOOTING`): the shaft still filled
  from its floor up. Replaced by the food door.
- **Nest upkeep** (`NEST_UPKEEP`: ants bite plants and seeds out of the door,
  with `grassroot` made cuttable at `penetration_resistance` 0.8): took the
  roots out, and the next filler took their place.
- **Founding through sod** (`FOUND_SOD`): no change.
- **A wider door while the old one is plugged** (`NEW_DOOR`, `9c302131`: the
  door counts wider for the roof and the heap cue while its mouth is filled):
  no clear gain.
- **The roof off** (`DIG_ROOF=off`), **the heap cue off**, **dig-down off**:
  the door stayed plugged; the roof was refusing 32-91% of dig attempts at
  home because, with the door shut, the only ground in reach is the crust.
- **The food door at a margin of 2**: §1. The clearance is now a gene, so
  evolution can narrow it.
