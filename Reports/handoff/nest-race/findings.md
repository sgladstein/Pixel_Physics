## Known traps

Check these whenever a new rule is built or reviewed.

- **A new carrier needs its own target.** Any ant that carries food inside the nest reads its own cell as home (first fact above). Give the role a target of its own, as `spoil_pace_target` and `store_target` do, and count how often it stands still inside the nest before scoring the rule.
- **A pellet in the jaws blocks digging, ~~eating~~ and `HUNGRY_OUT`, and in the mound it can't be put down.** (Not eating: see Retracted, 04:4x.) A rule that makes more soil carriers, or sends them under cover, can shut a door with ants inside who cannot open it.
- **Inside is not home.** Soil rules read `inside_nest` (ground overhead, or the founding shaft); home reads the dug home (from the ground row down). Say which one a new rule reads, and what it does in the mound.
- **Diggers walking back to their face won't clear the door.** A rule that counts on diggers to reopen a door competes with `FaceTrip`'s `only`.
- **The heap cue's door waiver stops at the founding surface.** A cut through soil over the mouth meets the full cue, and with no pellets beside it is refused.
- **`HUNGRY_OUT` is recruitment.** A rule that keeps hungry ants in, or feeds them inside, has to send new foragers out some other way.
- **A cell is a wall only if the movement rule says so.** Ants push through brood and crumbs (`PushPast`, both on); corpses, provisions, soil and spoil are walls. ~~`deep-trace/tools/doorseal.py` counts brood as wall~~: it counted brood and every food cell as wall until 03:50 and follows the walk's rule since. deeptrace's maps before then draw all food `f`, so they can't tell crumbs (open) from corpses or the heap (walls); the scratch patch `deep-trace` `home15/mapfood.patch` draws crumbs `c` and corpses `x`. Check a plug against the rule it would have to block before calling it one (see Retracted).
- **Check a default to 300k.** #637 measured as a gain at 200k and collapsed two colonies of four after 240k.
- **A map every 25,000 frames cannot time a seal.** Use `deeptrace mapevery=1000` and `deep-trace/tools/doorseal.py` before saying how long a door was shut.
- **Two arms of one seed are the same colony only until they part** (within 8-19k frames on this box). After that, compare across seeds, and don't read one seed's story as the switch's effect.
- **Count stays, not entries.** 28% of entries into the nest were soil carriers bouncing back within about 10 frames; merge re-entries under 50 frames.
- **`trip_deliveries` misses food handed to nurses, and `eats` counts bites, not food taken off the heap.**
- **`deeptrace` records every live ant unless told `ants=0`.** That wrote 353 MB of ticks in the first 60k frames of one run.
- **Food a nurse gives a larva is food no adult gets.** A fed ant's share is how hungry nestmates in the nest are kept alive (no grown ant starved on seed 1 over 30-100k with nurses off). Any rule that sends crop food to the brood (nurses, crop feeding, a store fill) takes it from that, and the hungry then go out and die in the pellet and false-road traps. Count shares to hungry adults and where hungry ants spend their hunger before scoring such a rule.
- **A lean ant can't dig.** Under half its grant an ant doesn't take its dig roll (`LEAN_FORAGE`'s `nodig`). Anything that shuts ants in, a sealed door or a pocket of the mound, kills the lean ones; don't count on them to cut their way out.
- **`HUNGRY_OUT` doesn't reach the mound.** Its way map starts at the door row and runs down, so a hungry ant above the door has no pull out; scouting is all that moves it.
- **A pellet holder eats.** `HAUL_BITE` is on. A pellet blocks the dig, the store load, `HUNGRY_OUT`, the hungry walk home, the nurse's crop feed (`crop_to_feed`), and its own drop while the crop holds food. Don't count "refused for a pellet" as food not eaten: at door food it went into the carrier's crop.
- **Maps written since 2026-10-06 04:32 draw crumbs `c` and corpses `x`.** deeptrace's `map_f` and `nest_f` draw them apart from other food `f` in the shared baseline (`deep-trace/baseline/`) and on main once PR 642 lands. A reader that lists map letters must add them, either as food (as before) or by the walk's rule (crumbs open, corpses walls). `scripts/deeptrace_world.py` and `deeptrace_dig.py` are updated in the PR; Laying's archived 617 readers (`heap.py`, `lostgeo.py`, `picture.py`) are not. Deep trace (04:47).
- **Door-shut maps and starver counts swing far more between seeds than between arms.** Baseline ecca174e6, eight seeds: door shut on 2, 10, 11, 0, 7, 5, 0 and 77 maps of 295; starved 12, 55, 41, 14, 47, 13, 5, 152. `MOUND_OUT`'s 4-seed totals (door shut 23 -> 41-48; starved 122 -> 77-151) sit inside that spread, and the direction is per seed: seeds 1 and 4 shut more in all three arms (2 -> 17-23, 0 -> 5-14), seed 2 shut less in all three (10 -> 3-6).
