The Guest Seat
Seat demo · guest · built and playable

You are a beetle living off an ant colony by smelling like it

The guest seat of the six seat demos. Ants judge you by how far your smell is from family, on a graded ladder: they walk on, stop and inspect, flare and back off, or bite. Rub workers and lie in the nest to take its smell, beg for food, and keep the disguise up, because it fades. Smell wrong and you lose your body a cell at a time.

Same colony, same landing, two players

Seed 1, the first 60 seconds after the rove lands at the nest (25 seconds for the raw guest), played by a script pressing the demo's own keys. The ring round the rove is its smell: red stranger, amber doubtful, green family. A mark over an ant is what it just decided.

The box is steady income's with the trained colony. The clock runs fast while the colony grows (about 25 s on this machine), then drops to normal speed when you land. A new beetle lands 3 s after one dies. The ledger grades your life: cuckoo (lived on gifts), thief (on the dead), predator (on the brood).

Twelve colonies, not one

Every seed, 60 s after landing, both scripts. The disguise is what keeps the guest alive, and feeding it does not change how much food the colony brings home.

The disguised guest's ants use every rung (walk on, inspect, flare, bite); the raw guest's almost only bite. On one seed the guest never reached the ants in either arm.

Worth judging by eye

  • Whether a raw guest dies too fast or too slow (about 12 s on seed 1).
  • Whether begging is too generous: the disguised beetle went from 200 to 700 J in a minute.
  • Whether the 1.5 s inspect and 1 s flare read as ants making up their minds.
  • The larva kill works and is tested, but is not in these clips: the script's walk does not reach the brood chamber.
