Nest Batch Ten
Teacher lane · nest batch 10 · 26 Oct 2026

The come-home habit survives selection, but 48 rounds of nest training didn't grow the nest further

Batch 10 started from the new "home, when low" ant and trained nests for 48 rounds, 32 colonies per arm, on far food that moves. The habit held the whole way: the garden starved 41 ants per colony at the end, against 311 for batch 8's control from colony 13. Nests were bigger and craters smaller than batch 8's (757 cells and crater 38, against 446 and 93).

But nothing beat the ant it started from. I tested the four best colonies over 16 worlds against it. The best for open nest dug 33% more open room (522 cells against 392, more in 9 of 16 worlds). It also starved a few more ants, brought home less food, and made bigger craters. So the new ant stays as it is, and the nest is still well short of the hand-built ant's.
