Sharpness Batch
Teacher lane · sharpness batch · 26 Oct 2026

Lowering the brain's sharpness did not teach the ants to come home in 48 rounds

The trained brain scores almost every step as a flat yes or no, because its sharpness has sat at the top of its range since the hand-built seed. So selection can't see a leaning, like "head home as the tank runs low". This batch lowered the ceiling to 2x and to 1x and let 32 colonies per arm re-tune for 48 rounds from colony 13, on far food that moves.

It didn't work. Neither arm starved fewer ants than the control, and both dug smaller open nests with bigger craters. The one gain was food: at 2x, the best colony brought home 24% more food than colony 13 in all 8 test worlds. It still starved more ants and dug a smaller nest.

Some colonies looked like breakthroughs in their own world (75-109 starved against about 300). Over 8 worlds they were ordinary. That was luck of the world, not a habit.

What I'm trying next

Sharpness alone doesn't make selection find the habit, but it might make the habit teachable. Copying the come-home teacher into the weights failed at 4x because the scores were pinned. I'm now repeating that copy at 1x, where only 31% of scores are pinned, and judging it on 8 worlds with the teacher off.
