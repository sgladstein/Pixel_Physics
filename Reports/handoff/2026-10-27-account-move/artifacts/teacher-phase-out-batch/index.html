Teacher Phase-Out Batch
Teacher lane · nest batch 5 · 26 Oct 2026

Phasing the teacher out did not teach the ants to come home, but it grew one real nest

Batch 5 trained 32 colonies per arm for 48 rounds on far food that moves every 20,000 frames. The come-home teacher was on at first and phased out by round 40. All test runs had it off. Once it was gone, the ants starved as much as the control: selection did not keep the habit. More colonies died out than in the control.

The arm that also phased out the old dig rules is different. Its nests grew round after round, from 199 cells at round 7 to 617 at round 47, and its colonies stopped dying out (4 of 32 at the end, against 12 for the control). Its best colony, below, has the biggest open nest a trained colony has dug with every crutch off, with a small mouth rather than a crater. Most of that arm's colonies still have big craters, and they still starve as many ants as the control.

What I think this means

  1. Selection cannot find "go home when low" by itself. The brain's step scores are saturated, so a small change to how it weighs home changes almost nothing it does, and selection has nothing to pick between. The habit has to be put into the weights directly: copy the teacher's choices into the brain, training only the home-sense weights (the recipe that worked for the far-from-home sense on 22 Oct), then judge with the teacher off.
  2. Colony 13 is worth keeping as a candidate for the nest. It needs a test over more worlds before it becomes the standard ant.
