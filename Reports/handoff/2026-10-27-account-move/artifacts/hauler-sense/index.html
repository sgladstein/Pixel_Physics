Hauler Sense
Teacher lane · why the nest is small · 26 Oct 2026

The nest was small because soil haulers wander. A "way out" sense for pellet carriers grows it, but craters grow too

Where the room goes: the new ant cuts about 1,000 nest cells in a run, and crumbs (half-eaten food put down) fill about 600 of them. The hand-built ant cuts about 1,450 and keeps 9 crumbs.

Making the ant choose Dig more often did not help. Underground it went from digging at 2% of its chances to 30%, but cuts rose only 7% and the open room shrank. The bottleneck is hauling. Half of all underground decisions belong to an ant carrying a soil pellet, and a third of its steps go deeper instead of out.

So I gave pellet carriers a sense of the way out. Each step reads +1 if it brings the ant nearer the door along the tunnels, and -1 if it takes the ant deeper. It reads 0 when the ant isn't carrying soil. It uses the same slot and the same trained weights as "home, when low" (both mean "this step heads home, and it matters now"), so nothing was fitted.

The open nest was bigger in 12 of 16 worlds, and the ants cut more in 15 of 16. Starvation was the same or lower. Less food came home (by about a fifth in every world), but the colony didn't go short. The cost is the crater: bigger in 13 of 16 worlds. The films show why. The nest is still one cavern, now wider, and its neck has opened into a funnel.

Then: nest batch 11, with the sense on

I let colony selection run 48 rounds from this ant to see whether it would trade the crater away. It didn't. More colonies died out in training than in batch 10, and the four best colonies were all worse than the starting ant with the sense: open nest 231-582 against 644, and cells cut 630-1,000 against 1,273. Selection bred digging back out.

Where that leaves the nest

  1. The way-out sense is the first thing that has grown the trained nest in a fair test, and it costs no starvation. Its cost is the crater: the nest is still one cavern, and a wider cavern slumps into a funnel.
  2. So the next problem is shape, not size: tunnels and side rooms instead of one pit. That is your third priority, and it now looks like the way to fix the second.
