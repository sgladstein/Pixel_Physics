The trained nest is a pit full of food

The trained colonies have about a tenth of the open room the hand-built ant digs: roughly 100 cells against 1,100. Two things add up to that. They dig about half as much ground, and they fill most of what they dig with stored food, 430 to 630 cells of it, while the hand-built ant keeps only 25 to 65 cells of food underground.

Same four worlds for every ant: the 448-wide garden bed, food 100 columns from the door, 200,000 frames, no selection. Each picture is 90 columns around the door, cut at the end of the run.

What I am doing about it, in your order

  1. Too small. The next batch, queued for the VM after the one running now, keeps only colonies whose nest keeps up with what they hold. Its size score already counts stored food and brood as needing room, so a colony can pass it by digging more or by stuffing less into the nest. Food distance is drawn at random for each heap every round, from 20 to 120 columns.
  2. Crater instead of a mouth. A crater gate built the same way comes after that batch.
  3. One room. Rooms come third. On the room score, colony 10 gets 0 to 9 and colony 8 gets 0 to 49, against 62 to 85 for the hand-built ant.
