Nest Batch Eight
Teacher lane · nest batch 8 · 26 Oct 2026

Nest batch 8 found nothing better than colony 13, so it stays the standard ant

Batch 8 started from colony 13 and trained 32 colonies per arm for 48 rounds on far food that moves every 20,000 frames. One arm was a plain control. One brought the old roof and room rules back and phased them out. The third did the same and also scored rooms on open space only.

No arm moved the colony forward. I tested the four best colonies on 8 worlds against colony 13. The best of them dug 32% more open nest (525 cells against 398, more in 6 of 8 worlds), but it starved half as many ants again (362 against 240, worse in all 8) and brought home a third less food. The old-rules arm shrank its nests again.

So colony 13 stays the founder. The next batch, now running, tests something different: lowering the brain's sharpness so that graded habits like "lean home as the tank drops" become visible to selection.
