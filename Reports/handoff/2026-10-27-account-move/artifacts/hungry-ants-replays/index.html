Hungry trained ants starve against the walls

Batch 4 tried to ease the colonies into moving food: in training the heap stayed put until colonies stopped starving, then moved more often. They never got there. The median colony starved 100 to 250 ants every round, even with the heap never moving, so the food never started to move, and the far, moving test showed no gain over batch 3.

A probe on hungry ants (below a quarter of a full tank) shows where they die. Most are not at home and not at the food. They are pressed against the two walls of the bed, including the east wall 300 columns from the door where food never goes. And in a probe of today's standard ant, the few hungry ants at home with food touching their heads walked away 32 times out of 39.
