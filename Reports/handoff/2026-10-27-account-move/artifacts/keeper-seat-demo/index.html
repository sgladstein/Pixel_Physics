Keeper Seat Demo

You are a hand over the formicarium, not an ant. Carry trained ants out to a far food station and the colony's road to it gets busier and the colony grows. The more ants you carry, the bigger the change. A switch on screen turns on a scripted "listening" that makes taught ants lean toward the food they remember. It adds to the effect, and it is labelled as the one fake.

How to play

cargo run --release --bin lab -- demo=keeper. The box opens paused at frame 6,000. The colony landed at the west end and has had time to dig a first chamber. Food arrives three ways:

  • a small ration inside the nest (3 cells per 1,000 frames, less than the colony eats);
  • a green-ringed station about 280 columns east, kept topped up so it never runs dry;
  • whatever you add with the lab's own FOOD brush.
YCarry. Press on an ant and release where it should go. If you set it down within 12 cells of food that isn't at home, it remembers that food. UTreat. Click an ant to put a mouthful of provisions in its crop. Away from home, it also remembers the place. PListening. Turns the scripted gain on and off. Off is today's ant, and the panel always says which arm is live. JOverlay. Cycles off, then who knows (a white ring for ants taught by your hand, amber fed once, red fed twice, grey asleep), then traffic (where heads have been).

The panel under the frame counter shows the counts: visits to the station, food carried home from it, the first delivery's frame, how many ants know it, your carries and treats, and how many times a place was passed on mouth to mouth.

What is scripted and what is the ant

  • Carry and treat are real verbs on the trained ant. Carry moves the ant's body. Treat is a real meal, booked as food the player made. Neither writes behaviour; the ant does the rest.
  • Listening is the one fake. It applies only to ants your hand taught (or that were fed the place), and only when they are carrying nothing. Each Walk target's pull is multiplied by exp(6 × FOOD_WAY) after the brain has already chosen to walk. It never changes whether an ant digs, eats or feeds. The trained pool cannot use the sense itself yet, because its weight on that sense was zeroed when the sense was added. Off, it is byte-identical to main (merged as PR #907).
  • The meal copy is counted but does nothing you can see. A taught ant that feeds a nestmate passes the place on at half strength. Copy strength 0, 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 all gave the same traffic. The share of ants that "know" never rises past the ones your hand taught, because a second-hand place is weak and the next stray bite puts it to sleep. It stays on as neutral.
  • The warm-up hides the landing. About 30 of the 52 founders starve by frame 3,000 on every landing tried, nest_goal's own included. The demo opens after that.

What tracing individual ants showed

  • Taught ants spend 67 to 70% of their time carrying food. The scripted tilt never acts while they carry.
  • When a taught ant is empty, it chooses to walk in 97 to 98% of its decisions. The verb was never the bottleneck.
  • One stray bite on the way home used to erase a lesson for good. Lessons now sleep and wake on the next bite at the taught place.
  • The demo's own bookkeeping costs nothing measurable. On the same colony it ran at 0.92 to 1.27 ms a frame with the demo on, against 0.88 to 1.15 ms with it off. A busier demo is slower only because the colony is bigger.
