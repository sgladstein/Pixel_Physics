Home When Low
Teacher lane · a new sense · 26 Oct 2026

A "home, when low" sense lets the trained ant learn to come home, and starvation falls by about five times

The trained ant walked out fat and starved far from the nest. A teacher that turned hungry ants for home fixed that while it was acting, but every attempt to copy it into the brain's weights stalled at about 45% agreement or leaked into foraging.

The fix was to give the ant a sense the habit can hang on. Each step it could take now reads +1 if it heads toward home, -1 if it heads away, and 0 along the way. That reading is scaled by how far its tank is below the cost of the walk back. A fed ant reads 0 everywhere, so the sense cannot change how a fed forager moves. It also reads 0 while the nest store is empty, so a colony that has landed half-empty with nothing stored goes out for food instead of walking home to an empty nest.

Fitting just the 16 weights on that sense to the teacher's choices raised agreement from 28% to 79%, with almost no change to anything else. Then the teacher was switched off for every test.

The catch

  1. The sense borrows a slot the brain was using for "the way back to the last food". That memory has to be off for the sense to work, and colony 13 does a little worse without it (starved 297 against 240). The sense more than makes up for it.
  2. The open nest is still well short of the hand-built ant's (about 400-470 cells against 1,000-1,400). With colonies now surviving, nest training on top of this ant should have something to build on.
