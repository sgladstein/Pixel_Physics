The Raider

You are a centipede, the shipped nest raider, set down beside a living ant colony that has had 20,000 frames to dig in. Pick off foragers, try the door, back away when they bite. The colony fights back with the same bite rules every animal in the engine uses.

Play it

cargo run --release --bin lab -- demo=enemy # the trained colony cargo run --release --bin lab -- demo=enemy oldant # the hand-built colony
  • ← → ↑ ↓ walk
  • Shift (hold) sprint, while your wind lasts
  • B bite, even with a full gut
  • E eat what you killed
  • Q put a crumb down
  • G cut through stored crumbs
  • Y season card · R new raider after a death

Your body is drawn in crimson with a white ring on the head; the ants are amber. The HUD shows one wound meter for your whole body. When a bite fills it, the cell that took that bite comes off, so a bite on the head kills you and bites on the tail cost one cell at a time.

After your playtest: no spit, and hit and run

The spit is gone: your bite works with a full gut, and the extra flesh is just not eaten. For strategy, the first of the three directions is built. Hold Shift to sprint at 2.5 times your pace (ants are slower than you now, not about even) for 4 seconds, then you are winded until your wind comes back. Lost tail cells grow back, one every 15 seconds, once your wound has healed and you have eaten enough to overfill your tank. So the loop is: dart in, kill, sprint out, eat, heal, come back whole.

Same seed, same scripted player, trained colony: walk toward the door, and back off at the first cell lost. Over 6 seeds and 20,000 frames:

Which colony is the better opponent?

Same seed, same scripted player, one minute of play after landing. The player waits on the surface for an ant walking alone, bites it, and backs off when bitten. The colonies differ only in the brain their ants think with. Which one would you rather play against?

Mutation at birth (your pick)

In demo=enemy every egg of the trained colony now takes a mutated copy of its parent's brain, using the training page's own mutation. The ants you eat leave no young, so your hunting is a selection pressure. Each raider life is a season, and the season card lists how hard the colony bit back per minute it had you in reach.

Mutation rateAnts left at frame 175,000, 6 seeds, no raider
Off16, 183, 274, 324, 326, 261
Training page's rate182, 2, 0, 0, 4, 72
A third of it319, 361, 147, 293, 128, 109
A tenth of it (default)253, 368, 307, 276, 336, 238
  • At the training page's rate the colony melts down: 3 of 6 went extinct with no raider at all. In the training bed, bad brains are culled before they breed; in the lab nothing culls them.
  • At a tenth of the rate every egg still gets a changed brain (about 3,000 in a run this long) and the colony lives as well as the unmutated one. That is the default. mutate=2 and mutate=3 choose the stronger rates; mutate=0 turns it off.
  • I have not shown that the colony gets better at fighting the raider within a session. The bite rate depends more on how the raider plays than on the colony, and a few hundred thousand frames is only a handful of ant generations.
