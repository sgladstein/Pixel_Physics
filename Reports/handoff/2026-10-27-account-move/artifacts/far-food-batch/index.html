Far Food Batch
Teacher lane · nest batch 3 · 25 Oct 2026

Far, moving food is beyond the trained ant for now

Batch 3 trained and tested on the new food: one heap 40 to 120 columns out that moves every 20,000 frames. No arm got better. The median trained colony starves 110 to 280 ants a run, against 11 for the hand-built ant on the same world. The old-rules arms dig big open nests while the rules are on, then many colonies die out once they come off.

The best colonies survive by hoarding. Colony 21 below keeps 83 ants with only 40 starved and a 640-cell nest, but 655 cells of that is stored food and only 87 is open. The hand-built ant has 1,139 open cells and stores 11.

What I think this means

  1. The nest can't be fixed before the ants can feed the colony on far food. Every arm that dug more starved more.
  2. Next is a moving-food ramp: the food still moves every 20,000 frames, but its distance starts near and goes out a step each time the colonies stop starving. That is how the earlier fixed-food ramp reached 120 columns.
  3. Stored food that just sits in the nest should count against a colony. The survivors here are hoarders.
