## What changes from the 00:30 sketch (04)

1. **Replace HUNGRY_OUT for young ants, not for everyone.** A long-run lean-to-forager shift is real, so instead of removing hunger entirely: hungry → eat at the store; staying lean for a long time → becomes a forager.
2. **Two cues for sending foragers, both local.** The store level (`larder`, already built), plus meeting returning laden foragers in the entrance chamber (Gordon). The second needs a counter, not a new sense.
3. **An entrance chamber is a real place.** Waiting foragers rest there. Nurses and nest workers go deeper. "Home" then has two levels instead of one door point.
