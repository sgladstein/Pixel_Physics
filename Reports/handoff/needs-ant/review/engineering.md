Reviewer: engineering lane (one of four). Document: needs-ant/design-doc-snapshot-2026-10-06.md. Repo: main 3ba1e7bd.

## E1. "One branch in creature_tick" is false: the brain's outputs reach about ten places in the tick, not two
- Severity: BLOCKING (the isolation table and "only the decision moves" both rest on it)
- Hits: Kept separate (the hook table, row "The switch"); Architecture ("replaces only the middle: brain, act and the walk"; "What stays exactly as it is")
- Evidence (CODE-READ, src/sim/creature.rs on 3ba1e7bd): besides `act` (7823) and `chooser_step` (7995), `outputs` from the brain also set: the step-or-stay roll `p_move` (7876, 7952-7955); the forage drive and its pace (7882-7905, gated on `chooser_for(..) == Chooser::TrailAway`); the launch impulse (7963-7964); the tumble on a failed roll (8014-8018); trail laying, `EmitA` and `EmitB` (8086-8091); the trace row (8154-8210). Inside `chooser_step` the brain sets persistence, turn and the pick's noise k (`Persist`, `Turn`, `Tumble`, 21925-21927). The costs the doc calls shared include the synapse tax, `synapse_fraction * start_energy * active_synapses` (7712), which exists only if the brain runs. The brain is also precomputed speculatively for every ant before the tick (`sensed_ahead`/`SpecWindow`, 7575-7603), so skipping it means editing that builder too or paying for a brain nobody reads.
- Consequences the document does not state: (a) "trail A as today" (Acts table) is undefined without the brain, because today trail A's amount *is* the brain's `EmitA`; (b) the "Persistence" term and the pick's noise k come from brain outputs today, so "shipped" in the steering-term table is only half true; (c) see E2 on the energy bias.
- Fix: replace the one-row hook with the real list of sites (step roll, drive/pace, launch, fall gate, tumble, act, step, trail emission, synapse tax, trace, speculative precompute) and say for each one what the new walk does instead. Price the work as a refactor of `creature_tick`, not one branch.

## E2. Not running the brain removes the synapse tax, which biases "starvation no worse" toward the new walk
- Severity: MAJOR
- Hits: Architecture ("costs ... shared"); Build order slice 1 gate ("starvation no worse"); Measurement (paired comparison)
- Evidence: CODE-READ creature.rs:7712 and 7799 (tax booked as `Account::SynapseTax`); assets/species/ant.ron:92 `start_energy: 200.0`, :265 `synapse_fraction: 0.0000022222222`, :147 `idle_cost_per_cell: 0.05`, :148 `move_cost_per_cell: 0.125`, :28 `tick_interval: 6`. INFERRED: at the doc's ~57 live weights the tax is about 2.22e-6 x 200 x 57 = 0.025 J per decision, roughly 7% of a typical decision's spend (idle about 0.10-0.15 J plus a step). MEASURED (python on baseline ledger.csv, s1 and s2): median ant life 7,051 and 7,226 decisions, so about 180 J per median life, close to the whole 200 J birth grant. The exact `active_synapses` per evaluation was not measured.
- Fix: decide and state it. Either the new walk pays an equivalent decision tax (simplest: charge the shipped tax at the founder's synapse count), or the comparison books the tax difference and the starvation gate is read net of it. Measure the real tax share from the colony accounts before slice 1.

## E3. The acts are not separable from the decisions: `act` is 1,899 lines with six brain outputs, 20 switches and 10 random draws
- Severity: MAJOR
- Hits: Acts ("The acts are the engine's own mechanics and do not change ... What changes is who decides"); Kept separate ("Touches shared files? no" for the module)
- Evidence: CODE-READ creature.rs:15848-17747, `fn act` (804 code lines without comments or blanks). It reads `outputs[O::Share|Feed|DropSpoil|Drop|Dig|Attack]`, calls 20 `*_of(world)` switch readers (lean forage, soil way, storeroom and others), takes 10 draws, and interleaves the hand rules the doc wants removed (`lean_forage_of`, `in_nest_for_soil`, `below_founding_ground`, `nest_within_reach`, `storeroom_of`) with the mechanics. `act`, `chooser_step`, `soil_way_pull`, `spoil_haul_target` and `dig_return_target` are all private `fn` in creature.rs (15848, 21762, 13820, 12012, 14047), so a sibling module `src/sim/needs/` cannot call them without visibility edits to the shared file.
- Fix: say which of two routes is taken. (1) Split `act` into mechanics (pub(crate) primitives such as eat, cut, pick up, put down, share, lay) and the shipped gating, as a refactor that must pass the identity check by itself before any new walk exists. (2) Or copy the mechanics into the module and accept the risk that they drift apart. Either way, count it as slice-0 work and drop "Touches shared files? no".

## E4. The kill metric "time in the dug nest" is mostly time at the top of the door shaft, so the doc's own failure mode can pass it
- Severity: BLOCKING for the slice-1 kill criterion and gate as written
- Hits: Risks (kill criteria, "time in the dug nest under 10%"); Build order slice 1 gate; Measurement (row "Ants live in the nest")
- Evidence: CODE-READ examples/deeptrace.rs:180-186: zone `nest` is `y > ground_y`, anywhere in x, with no test for room, depth or connection to the nest. scorecard.py:10-14 uses that zone as the headline. MEASURED (`python3 -I $SCR/nestwhere.py <baseline> 1,2,3,4 50000 300000`, joining colony.csv head positions with map_f at the same frame): today's in-nest share is 4.7 / 5.0 / 6.0 / 4.4% (s1-s4). Of that in-nest time, 55-61% is within 3 rows of the ground line, 88-92% within 10 rows, and only 1-3% deeper than 25 rows. 81-85% is within 4 columns of the door and 10 rows of ground; 10-12% is ants holding a pellet. So "about 5% in the dug nest" today is mostly ants at the mouth of the shaft. An idle kinesis on dark, cover and resting nestmates that piles ants at the door, which is the Risks table's own row, would raise this number and pass the kill point.
- Fix: before slice 1, define the headline as time in the nest proper: below the ground line by k rows (say 6 or more) and not in the door column, or in scorecard room cells. Report the depth histogram beside it, and gate on both the old and the new number, so that a door heap is caught by a number and not only by a picture.

## E7. Slice 1's "hauling and digging stay the shipped rules, wrapped as jobs" has no code shape that matches the rest of slice 1
- Severity: MAJOR
- Hits: Build order slice 1; Mapping today's rules (rungs 4-5 "job")
- Evidence: CODE-READ. Rungs 1-7 are not separate pulls. They are branches of one function, `home_pull` (creature.rs:21493-21574): store, harvest, store return, home, `is_nest_bound`, `soil_way_pull`, `spoil_haul_target`, `dig_return_target`, hungry, nurse-in. It returns one target. `chooser_step` keys one patience on that one target (`home_best_for`/`home_patience`, 21892-21910). The haul and dig *acts* are gated inside `act` by the brain's `Dig` and `DropSpoil` outputs plus hand rules (E3). So "wrapping" haul and dig means one of three things, and the doc picks none: (a) keep the brain running for `Dig`/`DropSpoil`, contradicting "the brain's outputs are not read"; (b) call `chooser_step`, which runs the whole ladder, including the hungry-out, mound-out and rest rungs that slice 1 replaces; (c) call `soil_way_pull`/`spoil_haul_target`/`dig_return_target` directly as target sources for a pull term in the new sum, and write new dig and drop decisions, which is already porting the jobs.
- Smallest real shape (INFERRED): option (c). Make those three target functions pub(crate) (behaviour-neutral). In the new walk, haul and dig are a "pull toward this target" term with the new walk's own patience. The dig and drop acts are new gates calling the split-out `act` primitives (E3). Say that slice 1 therefore already ports the haul and dig decisions, and only their target choice is shipped.

## E8. Switching on at 50k must make up per-ant state for about 400 ants, and the post-switch transient is part of what gets measured
- Severity: MAJOR
- Hits: Build order slice 1; Measurement ("Switching on at a frame makes the two arms the same world up to the switch"); open question 1
- Evidence: MEASURED (python over baseline colony.csv at frame 50,000, s1-s4): 371/403/412/460 ants. 72/67/43/118 hold a pellet, 132/113/95/140 have food in the crop, 94/100/96/108 are nest-bound, and 130-200 are under cover (zones nest plus mound_in), against 0-1 under 100 J. CODE-READ organism.rs:5515 onwards (112 fields): the shipped walk's trip state is `spoil`, `crop`, `trip_load`, `store_return`, `store_carried`, `dig_return`, `forage_anchor`, `home_best*`/`home_patience`, `scout_*`, `nest_bound_until`, `lunch`/`hungry_home`. Nothing records "the last open-air cell", so the new door vector starts at 0 for every ant, including the 130-200 under cover. Nothing carries building scent, so no heap carries it at the switch. Hold, job, dwell and the escape stall all start from defaults.
- Why it matters: the arms are identical up to the switch, but the new arm then takes a shock that the shipped arm never does: about 400 ants re-decide at once, with blank memories, in a nest the shipped rules dug. Early post-switch numbers measure that shock, not the walk.
- Fix: add a switch-time table, one row per new state item, saying how it is set (from which shipped field, or blank). Measure the transient (for example in-nest share and starvation per 5k after the switch) and exclude it as burn-in. State that any nest-shape result after a switch at 50k is partly the shipped walk's.

## E9. With mutation off, every ant carries the same thresholds, so the response-threshold model has no spread between ants
- Severity: MAJOR
- Hits: Genes; Jobs ("No fixed caste ... thresholds differ between ants (genes)"); Measurement (mutation off while measuring)
- Evidence: CODE-READ creature.rs:5797-5809: `mutation_off_for_measuring` means "births inherit exactly". brain.rs:188-200: only the 14 body slots get developmental (provision) variation, and walk slots are "the genotype as inherited". So in every measured run all daughters of the founder carry identical genes. Under s^2/(s^2+theta^2) with one theta, nothing separates ants except age (a fixed schedule) and the dice. Bonabeau's fixed-threshold result, low-threshold ants specialise, needs a spread of thresholds.
- Fix: give each ant a deterministic developmental jitter on its thresholds, drawn once at birth (or at the switch) from a new RNG slot keyed on the organism. That is not mutation, and it is invisible to the shipped walk because streams are keyed per (seed, organism, frame, slot) (creature.rs:7703, slots 106-213). Or say plainly that division of labour while measuring comes from age and stimulus only.

## E10. The no-veto rule is not built into the structure: a job's hold can outvote hunger, and gene drift can move the line
- Severity: MAJOR
- Hits: Needs ("How a need takes over"); Architecture step 4 ("built into the structure, instead of checked rule by rule"); Genes (need curves); Measurement (the no-veto guard)
- Evidence: from the doc's own definitions (INFERRED). Hunger at 30% of the grant is about 0.86 on the ramp from onset 0.6 to full 0.25 (0.94 after smoothstep). The hold "rises while the job makes progress" and has no stated ceiling. Any hold above 0.86 is a structural veto at 30% energy, which is exactly what the guard forbids. `hunger_onset` and `hunger_full` are genes. Once slice 5 turns mutation on, evolution can lower `hunger_full` until the 30% line no longer wins. The guard also asserts "positive weight toward food or out", which is a sign on a term, not an outcome: the weighted pick can still walk away when persistence or route hold outweigh it.
- Fix: state a hold ceiling below the urge at a named energy floor, and clamp the need-curve genes' range so the floor survives mutation. Make the guard assert the outcome in-scene (ate, or reached open air, within N ticks), not a weight sign. Test the gene extremes. Feasibility (CODE-READ): the test kit exists (`test_world` 27206, `ant_on_a_floor`, `ant_at_a_nest` 36216, `register_nest_site`; 7 tests call `creature_tick` directly; tests set `.energy` and `.spoil = Some`), and `below_founding_ground` (13172) reads only the nest site's `surface`, so every listed place can be built in a unit test.

## E11. The switch must be its own World field, not an env var or a `Chooser` variant, and the "check every match" defence misses equality tests
- Severity: MAJOR
- Hits: Kept separate (row "The switch": "`PIXEL_PHYSICS_WALK=needs` (or `World::walk`)"; "Known trap"); Risks row "Adding a walk enrols it"
- Evidence: CODE-READ creature.rs:19421-19430. The chooser's env var is cached once per process in a `OnceLock`, so an env-var walk cannot switch on at frame 50k. Only a World field can, as `world.chooser: Option<Chooser>` does (19433-19435). `Chooser::` appears 69 times in src (56 lines in creature.rs, 1 in specimen.rs): 34 `TrailAway`, 19 `Off`. Most are `==`/`!=`/`matches!` tests, not `match`, so the compiler flags none of them. If the new walk were a `Chooser` variant, every `!= Chooser::Off` would silently switch a shipped behaviour on for it (for example `fall_now_if_unsupported`, 7947, and the failed-roll tumble branch, 8005). Every `== Chooser::TrailAway` would silently switch one off (the forage drive, 7892; the away/scout term, 21994; the door reader through `reads_trail`, 21772 and 22071).
- Fix: specify `World::walk` (an enum: Shipped or Needs) as the only switch, set by the harness at the switch frame. Keep it out of `Chooser`, and branch on it before any `chooser_for` read. Replace "check every match" with "grep every `Chooser::` comparison and decide it".

## E12. Building scent on soil has nowhere to live: the cell has no room, and a position plane parts from soil that moves
- Severity: MAJOR (slice 2 depends on it: "dropped soil carries building scent")
- Hits: Scents ("Building scent on soil | soil an ant has placed | ... | new (Khuong et al. 2016)"); Kept separate ("New scent fields ... new file beside pheromone.rs")
- Evidence: CODE-READ src/sim/cell.rs:206. `Cell` is `{ material, shade, flags: u8, temperature: i16 }`, with no field for a per-grain mark or its age. INFERRED: a separate plane keyed by position stays where the grain was when the grain slides, falls or is carried, so the scent is left in air. Pellets do move: "pellet physics" is listed as unchanged world physics. Storing the mark per grain means editing `Cell` and every sweep that moves cells, which is the engine's hot path and touches all three games. Khuong's mechanism needs a *lifetime*, so one bit is not enough.
- Fix: choose and price one option before slice 2: (a) a plane of the frame the soil was placed, with no per-frame decay sweep, accepting the drift and measuring how far placed soil moves before it settles; (b) a flag bit plus a coarse age, as a shared-cell change with its own identity check across all games; (c) no building scent, with drops steered by the existing heap target.

## E15. The room count reads the whole nest as one room, so "a second room" depends on how wide the tunnel between rooms is
- Severity: MAJOR (the slice-2 gate and the slice-2 kill criterion read it)
- Hits: Measurement ("rooms of 30 cells or more joined by tunnels"); Build order slice 2 gate; kill criterion "no room beyond the first ever forms"; also the fix proposed in E4
- Evidence: MEASURED (`python3 -I $SCR/roomshape.py <baseline>`, which reuses scorecard.py:111-135's rule): on all 12 baseline maps (s1-s4 at 100k, 200k, 300k) there is exactly one room of 30+ cells. It runs from 2 rows under the ground line down to 28-50 rows, x 223-292, with 450-1,018 cells, so it includes the door shaft. Under the 7-of-9 rule a tunnel 3 or more wide has room cells down its middle, so two chambers joined by such a tunnel count as one. A 4-wide tunnel stub that is cut off by a narrowing counts as a "room" once it reaches 15 rows. So "any second room" can be refused by wide digging or passed by a stub. In-room time does not exclude the shaft either, so E4 needs a depth or door-column cut, not "room cells".
- Fix: define a chamber by shape (for example, the largest inscribed square at least 5 cells, or a width of 6+ over 4+ rows) with the door column excluded. Count chambers as the pieces left after deleting cells narrower than that. Check it on the 12 maps and on a positive control (two 8x8 chambers carved and joined by a 3-wide tunnel must count 2).
