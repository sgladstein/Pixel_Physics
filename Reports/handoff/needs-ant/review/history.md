## F1. The route hold "counted above the weakest option" is listed as shipped and used as a defence; it is shipped OFF and halved the colony twice
- Severity: BLOCKING (slice 1 ships "foraging with today's route terms" before the store exists, and the saturation defence rests on it)
- Sections: The steering law (term table, row "Route hold": "shipped, with CARRY_HOME=turn's floor"; failure-mode bullet "Saturation silences a term ... Counted above the weakest option it holds nothing there, which is what lets the home vector work"); Mapping (row HOME_SEARCH, CARRY_HOME); Build order slice 1.
- Evidence (CODE-READ, Reports/dead-ends.md:1260): the floor, "built 2026-10-05 in the deep trace lane's scratch, never shipped -- It brought carriers home and halved the colony" (lost pull 45-58% -> 9-26%, colonies about half, seed 4 dead).
- Evidence (CODE-READ, Reports/dead-ends.md:1262): `CARRY_HOME=on|fill|turn`, "shipped off". `turn` alone: "carriers left the heap after a median 21-38 decisions against 98-107, the colony took 15-50% less food off it over 150-250k, mean ants 100-300k 478/469/398/400 against 547/551/529/573, and 247 starved on seed 4".
- The condition its rejection depends on (dead-ends.md:1263): "Do not re-test by re-tuning either part alone: the cost is where the colony is fed and where its soil goes, not how the carrier walks. Re-test when: the colony is fed somewhere other than the mound and the door (a storeroom, food carried below the top rows), or soil carriers have a way out through the mound."
- Does the design answer it? No. The store job is slice 3 and hauling stays on the shipped rules in slice 1, so neither re-test condition holds when slice 1 runs. The Mapping row says "tested afresh", but the term table calls the floor shipped, and the failure-mode list relies on it to solve saturation.
- Smallest fix: term-table status -> "built, shipped OFF (`CARRY_HOME=turn`); halved the colony twice (dead-ends 1260, 1262)". In the saturation bullet, say the floor is not a proven defence. In slice 1, state that the carry home uses today's default hold (no floor) until slice 3's store passes, and that the floor is re-tested only then, per the entry's condition.

## F2. The history's central foraging lesson is missing: today's colony is fed by carriers who fail to get home, and three homing fixes each shrank it
- Severity: MAJOR
- Sections: Jobs (Forage: "Ends when crop full, when it turns to carrying home"); The steering law (Forage carrying home); Measurement (Foraging follows supply); Risks (no row).
- Evidence (CODE-READ, Reports/dead-ends.md:1258, HOME_SEARCH): "It shrinks the colony, because the carriers it brings back are the ones that fed at the heap ... a lost carrier lingers at the heap in the nest-scent fog (a median 98-107 decisions per carry, 44-52% fill their crop); searching, 73-98 decisions and 34-45%, so the colony takes less food off the heap." Seeds 1-4 mean ants 436/461/367/399 -> 257/341/12/388; 12 seeds, smaller on 9.
- Same mechanism in 1260 (the floor in the deep-trace scratch) and 1262 (CARRY_HOME=turn). Three independent homing improvements each delivered carriers sooner and each cut the colony's intake, because a lost carrier sitting on the food heap is, in today's lab, a feeding ant.
- Does the design answer it? No. The forage job is built so that homing works; nothing in Needs, Jobs or Risks says that better homing removes a meal the colony currently depends on, or what replaces it.
- Smallest fix: one Risks row: "Better homing has shrunk the colony three times because carriers lingering on the heap were the colony's meal (dead-ends 1258, 1260, 1262). The carry home is switched on only once the store job exists, and food eaten at the heap is counted in every forage comparison."

## F6. The gradient check's larva scent is not the shipped `larva_scent`, so its "readable on every nest cell" does not carry over; the shipped term is also inverse-distance, not inverse-square
- Severity: MAJOR (the design says gradients are used "only where measured", and this is the only gradient term it ships early)
- Sections: Scents in depth (field table row "Larva scent, fades within 10 cells"; bullet "A short-range scent from a source works ... readable on every nest cell"); Four ways to make a field ("`larva_scent` (13x13 box, inverse square)"); Each cue (Larva hunger "yes, within 6 cells"); What this means, point 1.
- Evidence (CODE-READ, src/sim/brood.rs:1264-1306): `NURSE_SCENT_REACH = 6`, a hard 13x13 box; sources only larvae of the ant's colony with `target > 0`, weighted by need `(target - energy)/target`; walls ignored; each source adds `need * (dx, dy) / d2`, whose length is `need / d`. So it is a need-weighted bearing falling as 1/d, cut off at 6 cells, and it returns nothing past the box.
- Evidence (CODE-READ, /mnt/project-files/needs-ant/gradient-check/README.md:18 and gradcheck.py:226-243, 263-265): the checked field is a solved decay field with "brood emit 1, walls sealed", every 'b' cell counted (eggs, pupae and fed larvae alike), decay length 3 or 10 cells, in float64 with no cutoff. - Evidence (MEASURED, `python3 -I /tmp/claude-0/hist/larva_reach.py`, the 12 baseline maps): share of dug-nest cells within the shipped 6-cell box of ANY brood cell (an upper bound, since 'b' includes eggs, pupae and fed larvae): median 0.86, range 0.64-0.94; it falls to 0.64-0.82 on the 295k maps. - Evidence (MEASURED, /mnt/project-files/needs-ant/gradient-check/aggregate.txt:60-88): the 3-cell version, which is nearer the shipped reach than the 10-cell one, has 1-10 local tops (median 4) and its ascent reaches the top pile for 0.62-1.0 of cells; the document reports only the 10-cell version.
- Smallest fix: say the checked field is a stand-in, not `larva_scent`. Either re-run the check with the shipped term (13x13 box, 1/d bearing, hungry larvae only, walls ignored), or change the claims to "readable within 6 cells of a hungry larva: at most 64-94% of nest cells on today's maps". Change "inverse square" to "inverse distance (each larva adds a unit bearing times need / distance)".

## F8. Fed ants with no returning foragers will sit idle inside: the forage stimulus has no baseline, which is how the rest pull's `all` form starved the colony bed
- Severity: MAJOR
- Sections: Jobs table (Forage stimulus: "today's `met` ... plus the ant's own hunger"); Needs ("Rest is not a need ... Idle ants are held inside"); Build order slice 1 (switching at 50k hides it).

## F10. The kinesis stand-in for "dark" (`under_cover`) is true mostly in the spoil mound, so slice 1's idle kinesis would hold ants on the heap, where the history's colonies were shut in
- Severity: MAJOR (slice 1 ships the kinesis with `under_cover` standing in for nest air)
- Sections: Steering term table (Kinesis: "new; `under_cover` and `Crowding` stand in"); Each cue ("Dark and cover ... staying under ground ... shipped (`under_cover`)"); What this means point 2; Risks ("Idle ants pile up where the first few stopped").

Caveat: the map writes some non-soil cells as '#', and `under_cover` skips organism cells, but the crop printed at s1 200k shows the covered ants inside the porous 's' heap, rows 37-59, above a door at row 60.

Add "covered ants in the mound against in the dug nest" to slice 1's idle-cluster check.

## F13. "Up" as a pull has pinned ants on walls before; the design weights it in four drives and relies on a give-up it has not specified
