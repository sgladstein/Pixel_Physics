## When each scent is laid (census, seed 101, 100k frames, 1.23M decisions)

Mean emit strength per decision (0..1):

| Ant's state | A | B | alarm |

| laden (crop > 0.05) | 0.651 | 0.316 | 0.114 |
| empty | 0.555 | 0.010 | 0.386 |
| empty, away from nest | 0.560 | 0.011 | 0.412 |
| laden, at nest | 0.618 | 0.296 | 0.042 |

- **A** is laid by nearly every ant nearly all the time: a "an ant walked here" map whose peak is the nest.
- **B** is laid only by ants carrying food: a real "I have food" signal (it is the trail lane's hand-written rule, crop +12, B -8, still exact in the pool).
- **alarm** is laid mostly by empty ants away from home: the pool has repurposed the alarm plane as an "empty and searching" mark. Nothing in this bed bites.

## Reading

- **A carries real, place-specific information, and the colony is built on it.** Deaf: stored food 441 -> 199, nest 432 -> 51 cells, tank 68 -> 19, alive 85 -> 69, all 32 of 32 seeds. Scrambled: stored 272, nest 123 (32/32). This is stigmergy working now. It cannot tell an ant reading its own trail from one reading its nestmates' (a shared plane has no sender tag).
- **B is listened to, and it trades feeding for storing.** Without B: stored 441 -> 528 and nest 432 -> 542 (29/3), but starved 41 -> 69 (28/3) and tank lower (23/9). Scrambled B goes the same way, smaller (stored 26/6, starved 26/6).
- **alarm carries nothing useful here.** About 690k units laid per run (billed), and no measure moves significantly when it is deaf or scrambled. It is a free channel: the natural slot for an emergence experiment.
- **No scent at all:** nest 42 and stored 361 (0/32), but starved 11 (vs 41) and tank 82 (31/1): ants feed themselves and build nothing.

Caveats: selection off measures how this pool copes with a change it was never trained under; deaf is off-distribution (the scrambled arms are the cleaner listening test and agree in direction). One bed (dist 100, mix food).
