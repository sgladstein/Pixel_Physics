## 1. The physics today

Three world-sized `u16` planes at cell resolution (`src/sim/pheromone.rs`). Every 12 frames (`PHEROMONE_INTERVAL`) each plane takes one pass:

- **Ground law** (`Spread::Diffuse`, planes A and B): each cell blends toward its 3x3 mean by `blend`, then loses `fade` of what is left (with a forced minimum drop of 1/65,535 so ghosts cannot persist). It conserves stuff, so a trail laid by many ants adds up. For a thin trail the blend is itself a loss: the line smears sideways and its peak drops.
  - A: fade 0, blend 0.25. B: fade 0.03, blend 0.25 outdoors, **0.05 in the lab** (the one existing per-world physics difference).
- **Air law** (`Spread::ActiveSpace`, the alarm plane): each cell takes the louder of itself and its best of 8 neighbours minus `fall` (12/255 per cell), then loses `fade` 0.35. It does not conserve: it describes a cloud around a source. It spreads at most **one cell per pass** (one cell per 12 frames), and because it uses the best of 8 neighbours, its cloud is a **square** (chessboard distance).
- An ant's three emit outputs write A, B and alarm (`EMIT_CHANNELS`, `src/sim/creature/mind.rs:69`), billed 0.0625 of a move per unit (`emit_cost_in_moves`). It reads the value of each at its head, the change since its last decision, and per-option scent differences (options.rs).

## 2. Measured: one ant-walk of trail, laid once (`examples/molecules.rs`)

120 cells x `DEPOSIT` (40/255), never re-laid; air rows are one bite (240/255) at one cell. Peak in old 0..255 units; width = rows at mid-trail over 1 unit.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| road | ground | 0 | 0.05 | 33.9 | 25.5 | 8.7 | 3.0 | 7 |
| today's A | ground | 0 | 0.25 | 19.3 | 10.4 | 3.6 | 0.6 | 13 |
| today's B | ground | 0.03 | 0.25 | 16.6 | 6.6 | 0.1 | 0 | 0 |
| fresh | ground | 0.10 | 0.25 | 11.4 | 2.1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| flash | ground | 0.25 | 1.0 | 2.0 | 0.1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| alarm | air | 0.35 | 12 | 27.8 (11 wide) | 0.4 | 0 | 0 | - |
| call | air | 0.10 | 4 | 141.7 (11 wide) | 49.4 (31 wide) | 0 | 0 | - |

All figures from one deterministic run. A colony round trip is ~2,200 frames.

What it shows:
- On the ground, **faster blend does not mean further reach**: the 3x3 mean attenuates ~9x per cell, so a fast-blending molecule just disappears. Ground molecules differ in **lifetime and width**, not reach.
- **Reach needs the air law.** The call molecule covers a 31-cell square in 180 frames; alarm at fade 0.35 dies at ~5 cells because it fades faster than the 1-cell-per-pass front moves.
- The lifetime ladder spans 60x: flash ~100 frames, fresh ~400, B ~1,200, A ~4,500, road >6,000.

## 3. Proposed physics: a fixed palette of six molecules

The laws above stay exactly as they are and are the same in every world. What is new is a fixed list of molecules, each a fixed parameter set under one of the two laws, ordered by one **volatility** number from heavy to light:

| # | Molecule | Law | fade | blend / fall | Role it can play (not assigned) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | road | ground | 0 | 0.05 | permanent network map |
| 1 | trail | ground | 0 | 0.25 | = today's A |
| 2 | news | ground | 0.03 | 0.25 | = today's B outdoors |
| 3 | fresh | ground | 0.10 | 0.25 | food here right now (lab box measured best at B fade 0.10-0.25) |
| 4 | alarm | air | 0.35 | 12 | = today's alarm |
| 5 | call | air | 0.10 | 4 | come here, from far |

Rules of the one law: as volatility rises, fade never falls; the first four lie on the ground, the last two go into the air. Today's three planes are members, so today's ant is one setting of the new genes. Flash is dropped (gone in ~100 frames on the ground; the alarm does that job better).

Possible later physics, not in v1: air reach drawn as a circle rather than a square (diagonal cost); a molecule that splits between ground and air (needs two planes).

## 4. Proposed genes (per ant, mutate like body genes)

- **Molecule gene, one per emit output (3 genes).** A continuous position on the 0..5 scale. The deposit splits between the two neighbouring molecules by the fractional part, so a small mutation is a small change. Defaults 1.0, 2.0, 4.0 = today's A, B, alarm exactly.
- **Smell follows the gene.** Scent input k reads molecule k's position with the same split. The brain keeps its three scent slots, so no new links (the link price line says links are the binding cost).
- **Amount stays a brain output** (already evolvable), billed as today.
- **Not in v1:** receiver sensitivity per molecule (body-genes lane found opening body genes let selection grow things food does not reward); per-molecule emit cost.

Why pairing emit and smell matters: a nestmate whose gene drifts stops hearing the colony and stops being heard, so a colony is pushed to agree on its molecules, which is the Lewis coordination game. Different colonies may settle on different molecules (private channels), which is the visible "signals emerge" outcome the owner wants.

## 6. Known risks

- Scent use has so far cost food (crowd trail silenced: 251 -> 363 food; trail grafts lost colonies 49 vs 9, gated graft lower 41/64).
- Training food sits 25 columns out in a fixed place: little information to signal.
- Chicken-and-egg: a new molecule pays only once both an emitter and a reader exist.
- Six planes instead of three: memory per plane at full outdoor size (~84 MB at 8192x2560 u16 double-buffered) and awake tiles.
- Turning the palette on drops the lab's B-blend override (0.05), which changes lab results by itself.
