What the mould got overnight

Everything you asked for last night is built, measured, reviewed twice and fixed. It is all in one PR, #1054, with the merge desk now; it lands on main once CI is green.

Where it stands right now

  • Built: food you can see moving, cuts that stay open, each piece eating its own food, the Wanderer, the Mould Organ, three challenge dishes, a dish picker, and the new look (the designer and I picked it independently and agreed).
  • Reviewed: review 1 found 13 problems, review 2 found 12; all 25 are fixed.
  • Now: PR #1054 with the merge desk. Waiting on you: whether the look, the Organ and the Wanderer feel right.

The new look

The glowing night look from the three options (B), with the labelled tool tiles and the storm timeline from C. Each tile shows name, key, how many are left and when the next oat comes back, and clicking a tile picks that tool. Holding a tool shows what it will do before you click. F2 cycles back to the old look and the other two options.

The Wanderer (W, or the picker)

Lead one mould across dishes. The food under it runs out, so it has to move on. When it reaches the crack on the right, press G and it pours through; what gets through carries on to the next dish. Full Wanderer page.

The Mould Organ (its own game: --bin organ)

A pulse leaves a heart pile and rings a bell at each food it reaches. Tube length sets the timing. Organ page, with sound.

The two reviews

  • Review 1 (bug check + adversarial review) found 13 problems and no crashes. The worst: Running Dry became unwinnable once all the mould went grey. All 13 are fixed, each with a test that fails without its fix.
  • Fixing them made the stars a ladder: "short" and "storm-proof" now count only once all the food is joined. Doing nothing now earns 0 stars on every free dish; before, it got stars on 2 of 8 seeds.
  • Review 2 found 2 serious problems, 2 medium, 5 small and 3 in the test tool, all fixed. The serious ones: after a dish ended, food that was still due drew a garbage countdown; and oats laid on mould that had just starved were refused after about 1.5 s, while the preview said they would work. Oats now rescue for up to 5 s, and the preview says when they won't. The tool tiles now work as buttons. The star table above did not move on any of its 72 runs.
