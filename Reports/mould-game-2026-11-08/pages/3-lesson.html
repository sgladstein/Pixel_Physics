Mould now teaches itself

After your play ("I didn't really know what I was doing"), the game opens on a briefing card and a lesson dish. Each step's banner tells you one thing to do, and the next step comes up once you've done it. Below is a scripted player going through it, one frame per step.

The question: would this have told you what you needed on your first play? Anything still unclear in the wording?

Each step stays up at least 4 s, so a fast player can't skip past a banner unread. That's why the scripted player passes one exactly every 4 s.

Also fixed in this round

  • Grey flashing gone. Tube edges flicked between fed and starving about 420 times a second; now 0. Grey now only ever means starving.
  • A slow reader's lesson no longer starves. The lesson has no clock, but the food used to run out: left alone for 200 s, 0 of 400 mould were left. The food now stays full until the storm step.
  • You can't get stuck. If you used up your salt or snips before their step, the step hands you one back.
