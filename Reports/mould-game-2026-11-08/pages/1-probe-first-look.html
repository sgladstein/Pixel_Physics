Mould: herd a slime mould with a lamp

A yellow slime mould spreads across a dish looking for food. You can't steer it directly. You hold a lamp it flees, drop oats it reaches for, draw one salt line it won't cross, and snip it. At 1:30 a storm strikes the thickest tube, and three stars score the network.

Does herding it feel alive, or does it feel like watching?

What I changed from the brief
  • The storm strikes one tube, not three. At three or two cuts, the toughness star was earned in 0 of 20 runs, even by a plan that spent spare oats on loops. At one cut it was earned 4 times in 20, never by doing nothing.
  • A cut is a line across the tube. A round cut was simply flowed around, and every run got the star.
  • Its own trail field. The probe touches no shared engine file, so it can't move the lab. The Seep probe can lift it whole.
