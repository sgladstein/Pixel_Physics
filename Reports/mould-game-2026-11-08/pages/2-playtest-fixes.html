Fix 1 · the lamp · dish 4 at 25 s · real time

Mould under the lamp halves in 0.02–0.07 s (9 of 9). Control, push turned off: never in 10 s.

The old lamp only made the mould turn away. Walkers that turn away leave the lit stretch empty, and an empty stretch is a cut. Every lighter setting of that lamp still cut the join. The new lamp carries the trail and the walkers outward, a cell at a time. It swings a tube a little less far, and it never breaks it.

  • Doing nothing now loses every time. The plan and the fresh player each beat it on 5 of 5 dishes (sign test p 0.06, the lowest five dishes can give). Before, it was 3 of 5.
  • Random play no longer ties the plan. 3 stars against 8. The aimed storm took away the toughness stars random play used to get by luck (3 down to 1).
  • The returning oats bought reach. The plan joined all the food on 5 of 5 dishes (2 before). Thrift got harder for everyone, as fix 4 meant it to.
  • The lamp alone still can't win. The shepherd and the no-oats plan never joined all the food. The lamp shapes the mould, and only oats lead it somewhere new.
  • "Middle minute frozen" means the food joined at 0:30 equals the food joined at 1:20. It counts dishes where nothing changed in between.
  • These hands are this probe's own scripts of the reviewer's strategies, so the before column is our replay. The reviewer's own numbers were close but not the same: fresh 7, shepherd 4, dump 5. Five dishes are too few for anything here to pass a correction, so read it as a direction.
