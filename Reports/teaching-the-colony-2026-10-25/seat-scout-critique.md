# Critique: scout.md (the lone scout seat), 2026-10-25

Checked at `src/` 1c8b7a3b plus six web searches. Verified: `mind.rs:539` zeroing (pool `layout=4`), `creature.rs:23015`, steer.rs no-old-age, 442/298/323, the 57% carrier (Dornhaus 2008), four times and two-thirds (press, Franks & Richardson 2006), carrying three times faster, and Sasaki & Pratt 2011.

1. **BLOCKING. The cascade, which the doc calls "the game", has no route to working.** See Fun #2 and build steps 5, 6 and 9. Three new things enter the brain: Lead as an option every ant has, the brain's "accept a tap" decision, and the per-ant faith value that gates acceptance. Each enters at weight 0. Under `PIXEL_PHYSICS_LINK_PRICE` that ships dead, the critique's §3 lesson. The doc hand-seeds only the memory layer (step 4). Teaching is also altruistic: the leader loses foraging time and the colony gains. Selection inside one colony may strip it even if it is seeded. *Fix:* for each new option or input, say how it gets weight: a hand-seed, a body-level pull with a heritable gain, or a training bed with far, moving food where teaching pays. Gate step 5 on "cascade beats no-tandem on paired seeds", never on "neutral".

2. **BLOCKING. Lives as chapters overturns an owner ruling and misreports it.** Decision 2 says "The queen body does not [die of old age], by your ruling." The ruling quoted at `steer.rs:32-34` is *"My player shouldn't die of old age"*. It is about the player, not the queen. Much of the design rests on reversing it: the first death at minute 12 to 15, "your twentieth life", and "Lives as chapters" as a keep-alive. *Fix:* quote the ruling exactly. Design the default within it: death only by hunger, beetle or drowning, and rebirth as an optional move. Old age becomes an ask, not a premise.

3. **BLOCKING. The A/B cannot detect the owner's objection.** In the build, the only verb that works through step 3 is the one the owner rejected: walk to food and an ant follows you. One follower and friction do not change what it is. Display ("5-8 nestmates ... file after you") is a column following you. Everything that escapes the objection is scripted in the slice: the "knows patch" flag, the fixed-chance cascade and the rival scout. Arm B, a whistle that pulls everyone within 6 cells, is Pikmin. It is simpler than the thing rejected, so arm A will win while still being "follow me". *Fix:* make arm B the scout minus its escape: the same tandem with contact and breaks, but no cascade, no rival and no meal-smell. If A beats that B, the escape is what made it fun. If not, the seat is an escort mission. Put the cascade's tap chance in the card's meta.

4. **BLOCKING. Engine "now" and "Works" labels that need new work.**
   - The channel table says "The follower's body ... **Works once**". The pitch says "On today's ants a tandem run is worth one delivery". But follow does not exist (Tap and Lead are marked new), and the steered body cannot carry an ant: `SteerInput` has only eat, drop, share, dig, bite, lay and trail (`steer.rs:61-84`). Today no run happens at all.
   - **Warn: now.** The steered body has no alarm key (no `alarm` in `steer.rs`). The lab's alarm drop is a god-hand tool. "Ants scatter" is not traced either.
   - **Taste: "the colour is new UI".** The crop holds energy, not a food identity. A named meal needs the plan §7 odour palette, so it is mem or new, not UI.
   - **Standing for daughters** ("she smells like you", P3) is unbuilt. Mark it new.
   - Steering is not wired into any binary; only `examples/queenprobe.rs` drives it, by script. Every steer "now" verb is engine-only until the lab app binds keys.

   *Fix:* relabel all five, and change the channel cell to "works once step 3 exists".

5. **BLOCKING. The head-butt rule misreads Seeley et al. 2011.** The doc says stop signals go "only for a site they have visited", and the Head-butt row says "only if you visited her site". In the study, pink scouts head-butted yellow dancers and yellow scouts head-butted pink ones: cross-inhibition from a scout committed to its *own* site. LiveScience's "only if that bee had checked out the opposite nest" describes the *dancer*. Nothing requires the signaller to have visited the target site (https://www.livescience.com/17395-honeybees-head-butts-decisions.html, https://news.cornell.edu/node/271186). *Fix:* keep "go and look first" if wanted, but label it a liberty. The nearest real basis is the foraging stop signal against a feeder where the signaller was attacked (Nieh 2010) [K].

6. **IMPROVE. The faked slice's "switched off" switches do not exist.** The queen rule (`creature.rs:23015-23020`) and no-old-age (`creature.rs:7947`) are hard-coded for the steered body. Worse, the queen rule in an established 80-ant colony blocks *every* member from laying while any other living member has `children > 0` (`creature.rs:22951-22963`). That freezes the colony's breeding for the run and confounds the card's "adults starved". *Fix:* list both as small code edits in step 1, not switches.

7. **IMPROVE. Step 6's dead-ends citation is stale.** "Nothing pulled them out" was true before `forage_out`. That pull for a forager inside has been on since 2026-10-15 (`how-the-ant-works.md:2026`, and the same dead-ends entry's "Replaced by"). *Fix:* restate the Display-versus-recruit risk against today's ant, which already walks recruits out.

8. **IMPROVE. Pace has no mechanism.** "More landmarks learned per cell walked" assumes something that does not exist: no landmark learning exists or is planned. The memory layer is two or three remembered points, and ants are blind by default. "Her memory bar fills faster" invents a quantity. *Fix:* cut Pace, or tie it to something real, such as contact holding longer so fewer runs break.

9. **IMPROVE. Tandem break rate and lost followers: true, but mis-sourced.** "About three in four" comes from a review summarising *Temnothorax* spp. Across species the rate runs from 19% (*T. nylanderi*) to 70% (*T. rugatulus*) (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00265-020-02941-x). "Lost followers still found the site faster than ants with no guide" is *T. curvispinosus* in emigrations (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00265-008-0565-9). It is not in Sources. Basari et al. 2014 support only the search pattern. *Fix:* cite the right papers and give the range. Make the break rate a tuning knob, not a fact.

10. **IMPROVE. Rain World supports the opposite of per-ant standing.** Scavenger reputation is one regional or world-wide number. Players report that an unwitnessed kill costs a little, not nothing, and the drift back is player lore (Steam threads; wiki unreachable). *Fix:* tag it [K] and drop it as precedent. The per-ant memory is the doc's own design, and it is a better one.

11. **IMPROVE. The minute-to-minute loop is an escort mission until the cascade pays.** Do the arithmetic at the doc's own numbers (one step per 6-frame decision, runs four times slow, 200 cells): each run takes about a minute or more, plus the walk home. That is 3 to 4 runs in ten minutes, and the walkthrough agrees. The reward for them (pupils who return, pupils who teach) is scripted. *Fix:* also time the slice with the cascade and rival off. If players stop enjoying it at minute 5, the seat lives or dies on step 5, and the doc should say so.

12. **IMPROVE. The clocks rest on an unchecked frame rate.** An 11-minute life and a first death at minute 12 to 15 both assume 60 frames a second. The lab has a speed ladder (`src/bin/lab.rs:23`) and a minimum framerate setting of 60/30/20/10 Hz (`:32`). *Fix:* measure the 1x rate before setting chapter lengths.

**Verdict:** the research is good and honest about weight zero for memory, but the cascade, death structure and arguing all rest on unseeded new options, a misread owner ruling and scripted stand-ins. As written, it escapes "follow me" only in its scripts. Fix the A/B's arm B and the build path to the cascade, and it becomes a real test.
