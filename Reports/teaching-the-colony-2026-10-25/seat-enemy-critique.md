# Critique: enemy.md (the enemy seat), 2026-10-25

Repo at `1c8b7a3b`. Most serious first.

1. **BLOCKING: verbs marked "now" need a steered predator body that does not exist.** The doc marks Try the door, Shove the larder, Coil and wait, Strike and withdraw and Let one go as "now". `sim::steer` drives "an ordinary ant" through five hooks in the ant's tick (`src/sim/steer.rs:1-27`), and is off. The doc's own build step 2 is "generalise steer to the centipede". The jaw and shove mechanics exist (`assets/species/centipede.ron:25-34`, `organism.rs:4217-4224`). The verb, as the player uses it, does not. **Fix:** mark these "mechanic now; verb new (step 2)". Scout their door is closer: a rival queen is an ant. It still needs steer on, on a second colony's queen. Mark it "new, small".

2. **BLOCKING: "Their scent comes from their own genes [E]" is wrong, and the open question has an answer in the code.** `blend_with_nest` (`creature.rs:11320-11360`) runs on every tick an ant spends at the nest (`mind.rs:2218`). Each tick it pulls the ant's scent slots toward the nest odour, and a child inherits its parent's *current* scent. So a stolen hatchling converges on your colony's odour. The doc's open risk ("whether a hatchling keeps its birth scent ... unchecked") is answered: it does not keep it. The scout claim reads blended scent too. The biology is mislabelled too. *Temnothorax* slave rebellion is enslaved workers killing the parasite's pupae: two thirds of the female pupae in Achenbach and Foitzik 2009 ([bioone](https://bioone.org/journals/Evolution/volume-63/issue-4/j.1558-5646.2009.00591.x/First-Evidence-for-Slave-Rebellion--Enslaved-Ant-Workers-Systematically/10.1111/j.1558-5646.2009.00591.x.full)). It is not hosts attacking strangers. **Fix:** recast the scene as an assimilation race: do the hatchlings blend in before a nestmate bites them? Rebellion as real biology needs strangers who neglect or kill *your* brood, which is new behaviour.

3. **BLOCKING: "Use it or lose it ... a real engine consequence [E]" is contradicted by the cited code.** The link bill is real energy (`creature.rs:11602-11613`). That same doc comment says "the training bed never feels the bill ... so selection has not shed it", across about 1,900 links. That a colony strips its defences once you stop hunting is a hypothesis. Nothing has measured it, and inside a session it is improbable. The Director bullet ("Stop entirely and the link bill strips their defences") rests on it. **Fix:** move it to risks and hopes, tagged unmeasured.

4. **BLOCKING: the Range's score breeds hiders, against "forage all game".** The Range refounds from the colony that "paid you the highest price", and price is seconds of stalking per kill. Fewer foragers out raises that price. The lone-predator report shows this. The +guard arm cut ants killed "by paying colonies that forage less: killed per delivery did not fall", and food went from about 6,000 to 3,300-5,000 (`Reports/lone-predator-2026-10-24.md:106-109`). The tournament's fortress-that-starves is the same trap. **Fix:** make the score and the headline readout price *per delivery*: kills per 1,000 deliveries, the report's own metric, or stalking time per unit of food carried home.
