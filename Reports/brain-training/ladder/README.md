## Headline findings (2026-10-17)

All measured unless marked otherwise.

1. **The hand-rules arm on the brain walk cannot put spoil down.** The shipped genome digs +70 to +88 cells on the game walk and +5 to +9 on the brain walk, where 6-9 of 12 ants end the run holding a pellet. So the stage 1 bar must be the game ant on its own walk.
2. **A lid over the workings still counts as room under PR 708.** A packed lid two rows thick reads 72 cells, and a nest sealed at the mouth reads 40 (measured by the independent review; one-row lids fall in by themselves). The drop-in in `dropin-nest-room.md` closes both, and costs honest digging nothing: 946 = 946 over 12 hold-out beds.
3. **Dig hold-outs are one flat bed with new dice.** The starting census is identical on every seed.
4. **Every death in the dig arena is old age**, identical for the do-nothing genome on all 12 of the rig's hold-out beds, so `alive` is a fixed handicap per seed.
5. **The forage heap runs out.** Trained seed 1 empties it on 6 of 6 east layouts. The score (food delivered home) still separates arms there, 21 to 38, but the heap caps what a colony can find, so it should be raised.
6. **The suite's forget floor fires on noise, about half the time.** The floor is tested per layout, so an unchanged game-walk genome takes the 0.1x on 3 of 6 mirrored pairs (a trained one on 4 of 6). The first version's 22% modelled code that does not exist.
7. **The stage 1 patch's own hand-built scorer does not pass.** It reads 12.8 room against 70.7 for the hand rules and 10.6 for a uniform pick, on the same 8 hold-out layouts, and it digs an open bowl with no mound (`stage1-code-review.md`).
8. **Births are on in the rig, with Lay banned.** The 1e30 `reproduce_threshold` cancels out of an ant's egg bar, and a banned `Lay` reads 0, above the hold line. Two colonies with food laid 9 eggs and declined none in 3k frames. Any `alive` count includes newborns. The brain lane was told.
9. **The shipped ant is already a war ant.** On shared food a game colony wipes out a colony that cannot bite on 12 of 12 runs, within 20k frames, from either side. Against itself one colony dies on 5 of 6 full-size runs. So F1's stability line must come from a pacifist pair, not the game ant (`fighting-rungs.md`).
10. **The predator rung needs no new body to start.** Untrained lone ants make 0-1 kills against the game colony, but that is the colony's bite: with it scaled to 0.1, beetles kill 17-29 a run and lone ants up to 92. Hunters must not breed, or a hunter family becomes a rival colony (`fighting-rungs.md`).
11. **A scenario timeline does not run under `update::step`.** A steady-income arena built from one needs `lab::scenario::tick_timeline` after each step, or the food never lands.
12. **Brood step A works, once moved out of the door.** Larvae in a packed side room, `births_paused` on, scored on larvae raised: the zero genome scores 0.00, the game ant 6.1-8.0 of 12. In the shaft the game ant raises all 12 on every seed (`brood-stepA.md`).
13. **The store rung has no pass arm yet.** The game ant stores only 2-25 crumb cells; a famine separates storing from not storing only if every body is capped at 200 J, and even then standing still is not significantly worse (`store-stepA.md`).
14. **The trained forager's founders starve as scouts, not as nurses.** All 16 of the champion's dead founders went out on the side away from the food, walked to the wall 124 columns out, climbed it before giving up, and ran dry on the way back; none carried, none fed brood, none drowned. The game ant walks the same route and survives it, because the champion burns 0.119 J a frame against 0.051. The brain cannot fix it on the game walk: forcing its turn output to the maximum when hungry saved none. No score term is needed, since founders_kept already charges each death (`forage-founder-deaths.md`).
15. **The give-up genes are not the lever; the brain's price and a hunger-aware give-up are.** Writing scout −0.5/−1 or patience −1 into every ant saves no founders without losing food. Priced like the game ant's brain, the same champion keeps 0.97 of its founders; a rule that turns a scout home after 40% of its bank is spent does the same and brings home 6% more food (`forage-walk-genes.md`).
16. **The forage champion is already the best digger but one, with no dig training.** It digs 90.5 room against the game ant's 76.0 and a dig champion's 96.5, and that dig champion forages below the game ant. The suite's current fold would pass a brain that forages one way and digs the other; fold per arena (`next-rung-forage-dig.md`).

17. **The reserve rule is a leash just past training.** At a fixed 0.6 it helps when the food is 45-95 columns out, then costs the forage champion food from 120 columns, and wipes it out at 170-200 (founders kept 0.58 → 0.06 at 200). The game ant holds up to 170. In the lab main test with the food 200 columns out, the champion's colony with it is dead on 3 of 4 seeds. Training never sees food past 95, so an evolved reserve will settle on a leash unless far layouts are trained or checked. A brain priced like the game ant's has no leash (`reserve-leash-check.md`).
18. **The forage champion's colony is half the game ant's in the lab main test** (about 153 ants against 319 at 60k, 4 seeds, every brain checked). The rig's ~8x win does not carry to the lab. The cheap-brain arm closes about half the gap (`reserve-leash-check.md`, section 3).

19. **Scarce food plus births reproduces the lab's ranking in the rig.** With 5 provisions per 1,000 frames and births on, adults × founders kept at 24k frames puts the game ant at 32-34, the cheap-brain champion at 8-15 and the champion at 2, on 4 of 4 pairs each. The champion collects the most food; it loses because it costs 1.75x per adult and lays half as many again, so 37 ants a layout starve (`scarce-forage-arena.md`).
20. **On the trail arena a blind walker is not a zero, and the worse side does not make it one.** Rest-only scores 0, but blind random walkers score up to 0.17 and a blind straight walker 0.20-0.26 on the worse side, because some of a group go each way and the walls turn a walker round. Scoring signed progress (time the wrong way counts against) puts every one of them at 0 and leaves the hand-built follower's score unchanged. A blank stage 2 brain is the random null, so every rung also needs a rest-only floor (`trail-rung-controls.md`).
21. **With the hand rules off, today's ant hardly digs, and a stage 2 ant must carry soil out.** With every dig hand rule off, the shipped game ant opens 3-19 cells in 12k frames against 75-78. Keeping only the rule that posts a pellet up the column brings back 25-81 cells of scattered digging. Corrected after review: only that rule and the founding shaft were tried, and most ants were cutting air, so "nothing digs without the lift" overstated it. A stage 2 ant must still carry each pellet out, because PR 713's drop rule has no legal cell inside a shaft (`dig-from-scratch.md`).
22. **The stage 2 notes' review (`review-stage2-docs.md`) changed four things the brain lane builds.**
   - On PR 713 a mind ant skips the jaw billing, so digging, gnawing and sharing would be free.
   - The trail bar is "reach the stage 2 follower (0.860)", not "beat it".
   - On the signed worse side, resting beats every blind mover, and the trail's bare end is a cue worth 70% of the follower.
   - The scarce arena's ranking rides on automatic laying, so food must cap births before stage 2 trains on it.

   Rung 2's flood must run through dug cells only.
23. **Once food caps births, the scarce arena ranks the champion last but cannot tell the game ant from the cheap arm.** Only one brake rule held eggs on this bed: income over a burn that also counts eggs and larva feeding, with the small-colony gate at 6 (the shipped 30 is never reached). It cut starvation from 29 to 6 a layout (cheap) and 33 to 13 (champion). But the game ant and the cheap arm then tie at every snapshot from 10k to 22k. The old game > cheap order was overbreeding, which a stage 2 brain cannot choose (`scarce-forage-arena.md` section 6).
