## What the ant training is doing, in one paragraph

The ant brain is trained by **colony selection**: 64 colonies of mind ants dig and forage in the training bed; after
each round the colonies are ranked by **lexicase** over a set of colony-level cases (food home, starved, kept nest
cuts, nest size, spoil left near the door, and later crater, room shape, lab survival); survivors seed the next
round. Every arm is **judged in a common garden (CG)**: the last round's top 30 of each colony in a fixed fresh world,
200k frames, selection off. The goal (owner, 2026-10-22): ants that **keep digging a large multi-chamber nest and keep
foraging for the whole game**. Since 2026-10-24 03:15Z every batch starts from the latest best pool (the
*ratchet*), never from an old founder.

## How to read the numbers

- **CG r47** = common garden on the pool of round 47. Columns are medians per colony of 24 ants unless stated.
- **nest** = kept nest cells (open, joined to the door, below the original ground line, roofed or in the shaft) by
  `nestcount.py`; **crater** = sky-open dug cells near the door; **shape** = `roomshape.py` (second rooms and side
  tunnels; one room of any size scores 0).
- **home8 overstates feeding** once nests hold food (colonies re-drop stored food as "deliveries"; in colsel17 about
  11k of 13k deliveries were churn). From colsel17 on, judge feeding by mean tank, stored nest food and starved.
- **Old ant** (the hand-built game ant) in the same gardens, food at 25 columns: nest ~302, crater ~0, starved 0,
  home ~716.

## Traps already paid for (each voided a batch or misled a call)

- `slay(x,y)` kills whoever owns the cell and ants overlap: cull by id.
- Mutation with prune 0 / grow 1 bloats a brain to ~2,200 links and the ants starve: use `mutation=neutral`.
- Score farming: ants cut surface soil 20-40 columns out and dropped it by the hole; only cells within 20 columns pay.
- A loop over a `HashMap` that touches credit or counters must sort first (2 of 64 seeds diverged). `events.csv` and
  `final/index.csv` rows are HashMap-ordered: compare sorted.
- A 60 GB VM disk filled mid-batch and every later job "went extinct" instantly: use `--disk 200`; a round folder
  with only `mutants.txt` crashed, it did not die out.
- Never pull a whole batch locally (19 GB filled the container): `evreduce.py` streams from the bucket.
- Launch from `main` once a lane's PR merges: its branch is deleted and a build by branch name fails.
- An inherited batch run before the inherited-nest fix (fa8fcc8f) started every round in a sinkhole.
- Pay rules cannot move *which* cell is cut (colsel11, 12); they only move *whether* ants dig.
- Colony selection runs rounds in lockstep, so VM CPUs idle at 60-70%: oversubscribe (~30 processes per arm).
