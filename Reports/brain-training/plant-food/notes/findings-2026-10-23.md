# Ants feeding on plants: first findings (2026-10-23)

Branch `claude/project-thread-o2tizu` (head 1e2dfb9b, on the Teacher lane's compute branch 89453ec9). Picture: `garden-vs-pile-s3.png`. Raw numbers: `batch-2026-10-23.txt`. Runner: `run.sh` (hcs founder x30, common-garden args, the brief's env).

## What was built (both off by default)

- **`plants=MIX[:SPACING[:BAND]] grow=F rain=R`** on `examples/inrun.rs`. It plants shipped species (default here: grass, herb, shrub, scrambler) every 12 columns, keeping 40 columns either side of the door bare. The stand grows for 6,000 frames with the mister on, then the colony lands. Add `cap=0` for no pile. It writes `garden.csv`: plants, seed bank, and food by kind every 1,000 frames.
- **`PP_MIND_GRAZE=1`**: the mind's Pick up can take food a plant still owns (leaf, grass blade, flower, fruit on the stem, a seed on the floor). It uses the game ant's own bite rules: a seed may survive the mouth and ride home. On the pile bed it is bit-identical (stock.csv and nestmap.txt, seed 1).

## Answers to the brief's four questions

**1. Can the bed host plants at play cost?** Yes, at about 2.3x the frame cost: 45-54 s against 18-24 s per 200k frames on a 4-core box. The stand stays healthy all game, with ~70 plants and ~1,100 leaf cells at 200k on every seed, even with grazing.

**2. Do current ants find and carry plant food?** Not without the switch. **The trained mind cannot bite a living plant or a seed.** Its Pick up was offered only on food no organism owns (options.rs). So it walks past ~1,100 leaf, ~90 fruit and ~180 seeds, takes about 5 bites, and all 24 ants starve by frame 2,600 (8 of 8 seeds). The old ant (`nomind=1`) on the same bed takes 712 bites (mostly leaf and flower) and all 24 are alive at 20k.

With `PP_MIND_GRAZE=1` (selection off, 8 seeds, medians against the pile):

| | pile | garden + graze |
|---|---|---|
| alive at 200k (of 24) | 22.5 | 0 |
| bites in the field | 2,674 | 1,174 |
| loads brought home | 286 | 25 |
| ground cuts | 246 | 46 |
| stored food at the nest | 41 | 15 |

The ants graze where they stand instead of gathering. A few become fat grazers (tank ~50) and the rest never find food. A leaf pays this gut 32 J against 778 J for a provisions cell, so a plant diet takes ~24x the bites.

With selection on (4 seeds), 0 of 4 garden colonies were alive at 200k, against 3 of 4 on the pile.

**3. Does a finite, regrowing supply stop the larder filling?** It stays small (15 cells), but only because almost nothing is brought home. That is not the healthy middle we want. Note that with this founder the pile colonies stored only ~41 cells, not colsel20's 389; those were evolved pools.

**The colony also stops digging.** Within 20k frames the stand seeds itself across the bare band and over the door, and its roots fill the soil (the pale web in the picture). An ant cannot cut a root: roots are plant tissue, and the jaw only cuts ground. Cuts fall from 246 to 46. This breaks the dig-all-game rule unless the yard around the door stays clear.

**4. What does selection need to change?**
- Delivery pays per cell (`w_deliver` x price). A leaf cell pays the same as a provisions cell holding 24x the energy, so pay must be per joule (the cell's worth) on a plant bed.
- The founder has never seen plant food, so a garden-only round kills every colony before selection can act. Something must keep colonies alive while grazing and carrying are learned.

## Proposed design (for the owner's card)

1. Land the two switches via the merge desk, off by default.
2. **Weaning bed**: garden plus the pile, with the pile's cap falling round by round (e.g. 60 -> 0 over N rounds), and delivery paid per joule. Colonies live while selection finds ants that graze and carry plant food home. Judge on tank_mean, nest_food, starved, and nest/cuts as usual.
3. **Keep a yard around the door.** Recommended: the harness pulls seedlings within ~30 columns of the door, a training scaffold like the pile top-up. Alternatives: a 512-wide bed (~2x the cost again), or letting ants cut roots, which is a physics change for every world.
4. Then run the Brood and breeding lane's `brood=lay` on it for boom and bust.

## Weaning bed, first look (owner chose it 17:11Z)

Branch head 7eb4f68a. It adds `fjoule=1`, `weed=B` and `cap_to=C:F` (commit d7a5f455), merges the Brood lane's `brood=lay` (3f25e6f1), and is bit-identical on the pile bed without the new args. Every arm below is garden + `PP_MIND_GRAZE=1` + `weed=30` + `fjoule=1`, hcs x30, selection off, 4 seeds, 200k frames, medians. Picture: `weaning-bed-s3.png`. Numbers: `batch-weaning-2026-10-23.txt`.

| pile cap | alive at 200k | bites | loads home | cuts | stored |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60, no plants (today; 8 seeds) | 22.5 | 2,674 | 286 | 246 | 41 |
| 30, constant | 18 | 2,600 | 219 | 253 | 15 |
| 15, constant | 1.5 | 1,626 | 134 | 193 | 15 |
| 0 (plants only) | 0 | 441 | 18 | 72 | 4 |
| 60 falling to 0 by 150k | 1 (colonies die after the pile runs out) | 2,125 | 250 | 230 | 16 |
| same, without weed | 0.5 | 1,691 | 136 | 102 | 41 |
| same, selection on | 0 of 4 | 2,283 | 206 | 252 | 3 |

- **Weeding brings digging back**: 230 cuts against 102 without it (the pile bed: 246).
- **A 30-cell pile is the deepest step today's founder survives in one round.** At 15 the colony collapses. A within-round fall to 0 just delays the collapse until the pile is gone, and in-run selection does not find plant foragers in time.
- So the weaning has to happen **across rounds, in colony selection**. Start at cap 30, and lower the cap only when a round's colonies hold their numbers. For example, drop 5 when the median alive is at least 20 of 24, and hold otherwise. colsel passes `cap=` per round.
- **Laying (`brood=lay`) on the weaning bed booms then crashes.** At cap 15 the colony peaks at 57-91 adults by 20k and is gone by ~100-140k on 4 of 4 seeds. `lay_cap=48:8` lowers the peak (67) but does not stop the crash. On plants only, a colony with laying dies before 20k.

**Note (17:55Z, after the Brood lane's hungry probe):** at cap 15 (no laying, selection off) the ants starve *beside food*. At each starvation, the nearest stock row shows a median of 14–27 food cells stored at home and 41–48 in the field (the two capped piles included), on 4 of 4 seeds. The cap-15 collapse is therefore at least partly the evolved mind not eating stored food when hungry (the Brood lane measured it choosing Walk 200 of 214 times beside crumbs), not only too little food. On plants only, by contrast, food at home is 2–8 cells.
